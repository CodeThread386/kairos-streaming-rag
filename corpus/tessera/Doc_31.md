# Event Cancellation, Postponement and Force Majeure

## §1 Who decides
The event owner decides to cancel or postpone a customer event after consulting the sponsoring Director. The decision is recorded in EventHub.

## §2 Notifying attendees and vendors
Attendees must be notified within 24 hours of the decision, and the venue, caterers and AV vendors must be informed the same day so that cancellation charges are kept to a minimum.

## §3 Cancellation charges
Cancellation charges are paid from the event's cost centre. Non-refundable charges above ₹50,000 need Director approval before they are paid.

## §4 Using venue cancellation terms
Before cancelling, the organiser must check the venue's cancellation and refund terms, since moving the date is often cheaper than cancelling. Venue-specific terms are listed in the venue cancellation documents for each city.

## §5 Event insurance
Events with a total budget above ₹10,00,000 must be covered by event cancellation insurance, arranged through Finance at least 30 days before the event.
