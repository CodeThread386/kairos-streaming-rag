# Gifts, Entertainment and Hospitality Policy

## §1 Customer entertainment limits
Business meals and entertainment for customers are limited to ₹5,000 per person per event. Spending above this limit needs VP approval before the event.

## §2 Government officials
Any gift, meal or hospitality for a government official needs written pre-approval from Legal, regardless of value.

## §3 Gifts to customers
Gifts to customers must not exceed ₹3,000 in value per person per year and must carry Tessera branding where possible. Cash and gift cards are never allowed.

## §4 Gifts received
Gifts received from vendors or customers above ₹2,000 must be declared in the gift register within 7 days.
