# Team Offsite Guidelines

## §1 Budget
Internal team offsites are budgeted at ₹3,000 per person per day, including venue, food and activities. Offsites are limited to one per team per year.

## §2 Approvals
Offsites are approved by the department head. Overnight offsites need VP approval.

## §3 Venues and travel
Offsites should use Tessera offices or partner venues from the city venue catalogs where possible. Travel to an offsite location follows the domestic travel rules.

## §4 Alcohol
Alcohol is not paid for by the company at team offsites.
