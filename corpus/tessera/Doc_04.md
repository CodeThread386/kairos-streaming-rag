# Travel Booking Rules and Exceptions

## §1 Booking through TravelHub
All flights, trains and hotels are booked through TravelHub, the corporate travel desk. Domestic trips should be booked at least 14 days before departure and international trips at least 21 days before departure to secure the lowest logical fare.

## §2 Late bookings
A booking made less than 7 days before departure must include a business justification in TravelHub, which the reporting manager confirms. If the selected fare is more than 20% above the lowest logical fare shown by TravelHub, Director approval is also required.

## §3 Post-travel booking exception
Travel booked outside TravelHub, or booked and claimed after the travel has already taken place, is a post-travel booking. Post-travel bookings are reimbursed only as an exception: they require Senior Director approval and a written justification explaining why TravelHub could not be used. The claim must be filed within 10 business days of the trip ending, and reimbursement is capped at the TravelHub fare that would have applied on the original travel date.

## §4 Combining personal and business travel
Employees may extend a business trip for personal reasons if the extension does not raise the cost to the company. Any fare difference and the hotel nights for personal days are paid by the employee.

## §5 Cancellations and no-shows
Employees must cancel unused bookings in TravelHub as soon as plans change. No-show charges are reimbursed only when the trip was cancelled for business reasons confirmed by the reporting manager.
