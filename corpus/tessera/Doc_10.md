# Event Planning Policy — Customer Workshops

## §1 What counts as a customer workshop
A customer workshop is an in-person session hosted by Tessera for current or prospective customers, such as product trainings, design sprints and executive briefings. Internal team offsites are covered by the Team Offsite Guidelines instead.

## §2 Registration and lead time
Every customer workshop must be registered in EventHub at least 20 business days before the event date. Registration needs the agenda, the expected headcount, the venue shortlist and the cost centre.

## §3 Budget per attendee
The standard budget for a customer workshop is ₹4,000 per attendee per day, covering venue, catering and materials. Spending above this needs approval under the Approval Matrix.

## §4 Approvals by total budget
Workshops with a total budget up to ₹2,50,000 are approved by a Director. Budgets between ₹2,50,000 and ₹10,00,000 need VP approval, and budgets above ₹10,00,000 need CXO approval.

## §5 Customer data and consent
Attendee lists may be shared with venues only to the extent needed for entry and catering. Photography at customer workshops needs written consent from each attendee.
