# Sustainable Travel Guidelines

## §1 Travel only when needed
Employees should consider a video call before planning any trip. Internal meetings that can be held online should not require travel.

## §2 Preferring rail
For journeys where the train takes under 4 hours, rail travel is preferred over flying.

## §3 Carbon reporting
TravelHub records the estimated carbon emissions of every booking, and each function receives a quarterly emissions report.
