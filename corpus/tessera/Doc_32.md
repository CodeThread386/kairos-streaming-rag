# Customer Workshop Playbook

## §1 Four weeks before
Define the workshop objectives and the target audience, register the event in EventHub, and shortlist two or three venues from the city venue catalog.

## §2 Three weeks before
Book the venue and send invitations. Ask invitees for dietary and accessibility needs in the registration form.

## §3 One week before
Order AV equipment and print materials. Share the confirmed agenda with speakers and the venue.

## §4 Two days before
Confirm the final headcount with the caterer, adding a buffer of about 5% for walk-ins, and share the attendee list with the venue for entry.

## §5 After the workshop
Send a feedback form within 2 days, upload slides and recordings to the Events drive, and close all invoices within 30 days.
