# Hotel Booking and Preferred Hotels

## §1 Preferred hotel program
Tessera has negotiated rates with preferred hotels in Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Singapore and Berlin. Preferred hotels are shown first in TravelHub and include breakfast and Wi-Fi.

## §2 Exceeding the hotel limit
A hotel stay up to 20% above the city limit needs Director approval. Stays more than 20% above the limit need VP approval.

## §3 Home stays and serviced apartments
Home-sharing rentals are not allowed for business travel. Serviced apartments may be booked through TravelHub for stays of 7 nights or longer.

## §4 Early check-in and late check-out
Early check-in or late check-out charges are reimbursed when the flight timing makes them necessary.
