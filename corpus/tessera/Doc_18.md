# Event Accessibility and Safety Guidelines

## §1 Accessibility requirements
Every venue used for a customer event must offer step-free access to the main session room. Organisers must ask about accessibility needs during registration and share them with the venue at least 5 days before the event.

## §2 First aid and medical support
A first-aid kit must be available at every event, and events with more than 50 attendees need a trained first-aider on site.

## §3 Fire safety briefing
At the start of each event, the host must point out fire exits and the assembly point.

## §4 Emergency contacts
The event organiser must share the venue manager's number and the Tessera security helpline with all staff working at the event.
