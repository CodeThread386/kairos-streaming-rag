# Domestic Travel Reimbursement Rules

## §1 Standard reimbursement rule
Employees are reimbursed for actual, reasonable and necessary business travel expenses that stay within the limits in this policy. Every claim must be supported by receipts and submitted in ExpenseHub within 30 days of returning from the trip. Expenses above a limit are reimbursed only up to that limit unless an exception was approved before the expense was incurred.

## §2 Air and rail travel
Domestic flights are booked in economy class through TravelHub. Rail travel in AC 2-tier is allowed for any trip, and AC first class is allowed for overnight journeys. For trips where the train journey is under 4 hours, rail is preferred over flying.

## §3 Hotel limits by city tier
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to ₹8,000 per night in Tier A cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR and Bengaluru), up to ₹6,500 per night in Tier B cities (Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kolkata), and up to ₹4,500 per night in all other cities. Limits include taxes but exclude breakfast charged separately.

## §4 Local conveyance
Airport and station transfers by ride-hailing app or metered taxi are reimbursed at actual cost. Self-driven personal vehicles are reimbursed at ₹12 per km for cars and ₹5 per km for two-wheelers, with a trip log.

## §5 Non-reimbursable items
Alcohol, minibar charges, in-room movies, spa services, traffic fines and personal laundry on trips shorter than five nights are not reimbursed.
