# Mobile Phone and Internet Reimbursement

## §1 Monthly mobile allowance
Employees receive a mobile allowance of ₹1,000 per month, and managers receive ₹1,500 per month, paid with salary.

## §2 International roaming
International roaming packs for business trips are reimbursed up to ₹5,000 per trip. Employees should buy the operator's roaming pack before departure rather than paying per-minute charges.

## §3 Local SIM cards abroad
A local SIM card bought abroad for a business trip is reimbursed with a receipt, as an alternative to a roaming pack.
