# Venue Cancellation and Refund Terms — Bengaluru and Hyderabad Partner Venues

## §1 Lakeview Convention Centre, Bengaluru
Lakeview Convention Centre refunds the full deposit for cancellations made at least 30 days before the event. Cancelling between 30 and 10 days before costs 40% of the contract value, and cancelling within 10 days costs 100%.

## §2 The Indigo Loft, Bengaluru
The Indigo Loft allows free cancellation up to 5 days before the event. Cancelling within 5 days costs 50% of the hire charge.

## §3 Pearl Residency Hotel, Hyderabad
Pearl Residency allows free cancellation up to 10 days before the event. Cancelling between 10 and 3 days before costs 30% of the package value, and cancelling within 3 days costs 100%.

## §4 Internal rooms in Bengaluru and Hyderabad
Internal Tessera rooms can be cancelled without charge, but bookings must be released at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a no-show flag.
