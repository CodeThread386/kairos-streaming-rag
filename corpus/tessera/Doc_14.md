# Venue Catalog — Hyderabad

## §1 How to use this catalog
This catalog lists approved venues for meetings, trainings and customer workshops in Hyderabad. Capacities are given for classroom and theatre layouts.

## §2 Tessera Hyderabad Office, HITEC City
The Golconda Room seats 28 in classroom layout and 40 in theatre layout. The Charminar Boardroom seats 14. Both rooms include projectors and video conferencing.

## §3 Pearl Residency Hotel, Gachibowli
The Emerald Ballroom seats 100 in theatre layout and 60 in classroom layout. The Topaz Room seats 25 in classroom layout. The day delegate package costs ₹2,200 per person and includes lunch, tea breaks and a projector. Pearl Residency allows only in-house catering.

## §4 Booking procedure
Venue requests are raised with the Events Desk in the WorkSpace app at least 15 business days before the event. Pearl Residency holds a provisional booking for 5 days.
