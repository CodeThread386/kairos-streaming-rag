# Ground Transportation and Cabs

## §1 Company cabs
Company cabs are available in Bengaluru, Pune and Hyderabad for employees whose shifts end between 9 PM and 6 AM. Cabs are booked through the WorkSpace app at least 2 hours in advance.

## §2 Late-night safety
For any trip ending after 10 PM, women employees are dropped first or last as they prefer, and every company cab carries a GPS tracker and an SOS button. Security receives a trip report for every late-night drop.

## §3 Ride-hailing on business
Ride-hailing apps can be used for business travel within a city, including customer visits and airport transfers. Premium ride categories are reimbursed only when no standard ride is available, with a screenshot as proof.

## §4 Own vehicle
Employees who use their own car for business are reimbursed at ₹12 per km and those using a two-wheeler at ₹5 per km. Parking and tolls are reimbursed at actual cost with receipts.

## §5 Rental cars
Self-drive rental cars are allowed only for international trips when public transport is impractical, and must include full insurance cover.
