# Information Security — Data Handling

## §1 Data classification
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer data is always at least Confidential.

## §2 Sharing data with vendors
Confidential data may be shared with a vendor, such as a venue or caterer, only if the vendor has signed a non-disclosure agreement and only the minimum data needed is shared. Attendee lists shared with venues should include names only, not phone numbers or emails, unless the venue needs them for entry.

## §3 Printing and physical documents
Confidential documents may be printed only on secure office printers and must be shredded after use.

## §4 Reporting incidents
Suspected data leaks must be reported to the Security Office within 2 hours of discovery.
