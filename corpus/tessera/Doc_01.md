# Travel & Expense Policy — Overview and Scope

## §1 Purpose
This policy sets out how Tessera Labs employees plan, book, approve and claim business travel and related expenses. It aims to keep travel safe, cost-effective and auditable while treating employees fairly.

## §2 Who the policy applies to
The policy applies to all full-time employees, interns and fixed-term contractors of Tessera Labs in India. Employees on international assignment follow the host-country addendum where one exists. Customers and vendors travelling at Tessera's request are covered only when a purchase order explicitly says so.

## §3 Domestic and international trips
A domestic trip is any business trip that starts and ends in India. An international trip is any business trip where at least one leg leaves India, including trips to Nepal, Bhutan and Sri Lanka. A trip that mixes domestic and international legs is treated as international for approvals and receipts.

## §4 Related documents
Reimbursement limits for domestic trips are in the Domestic Travel Reimbursement Rules. International trips additionally follow the International Travel Policy. Booking timelines and exceptions are covered in Travel Booking Rules and Exceptions. Claim filing and receipts are covered in Expense Claims and Receipts. Approval authorities are listed in the Approval Matrix.

## §5 Policy violations
Claims that break this policy may be rejected or recovered from salary. Repeated or deliberate violations, such as splitting bills to stay under limits, are reported to HR and may lead to disciplinary action.
