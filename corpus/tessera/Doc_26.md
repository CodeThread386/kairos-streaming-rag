# Travel Insurance and Medical Emergencies Abroad

## §1 Coverage
Every international business traveller is covered by the group travel insurance policy for medical emergencies up to USD 250,000, emergency evacuation, trip interruption and loss of baggage. No enrolment is needed.

## §2 Medical emergencies
In a medical emergency abroad, the traveller or a colleague should call the 24x7 assistance helpline printed on the insurance card. The insurer arranges cashless treatment at network hospitals.

## §3 Claims for out-of-pocket costs
Out-of-pocket medical costs are claimed from the insurer within 30 days, with original bills and the doctor's report. These costs are not claimed through ExpenseHub.

## §4 Pre-existing conditions
Pre-existing medical conditions are covered for emergencies only. Travellers with ongoing treatment should carry a doctor's letter.
