# Venue Catalog — Bengaluru

## §1 How to use this catalog
This catalog lists approved venues for meetings, trainings and customer workshops in Bengaluru. Capacities are given for classroom, theatre and boardroom layouts.

## §2 Tessera Bengaluru Campus, Whitefield
The Nandi Auditorium seats 120 in theatre layout. The Cubbon Training Room seats 36 in classroom layout and 48 in theatre layout. The Ulsoor Boardroom seats 16. Video conferencing and recording are included in every room.

## §3 Lakeview Convention Centre, Hebbal
The Banyan Hall seats 150 in theatre layout and 90 in classroom layout, and it can be split into two halls of 75. The day delegate package costs ₹3,100 per person, including lunch and two tea breaks. The minimum booking is 40 guests.

## §4 The Indigo Loft, Indiranagar
The Indigo Loft is a creative studio that seats 30 in workshop layout with movable furniture and whiteboard walls. Hire costs ₹38,000 per day, and external catering from approved vendors is allowed at no extra fee.

## §5 Booking procedure
Venue requests are raised with the Events Desk in the WorkSpace app at least 15 business days before the event. Lakeview Convention Centre requires a signed contract 30 days before the event.
