# Meeting Room Booking in Tessera Offices

## §1 How to book
Meeting rooms in Tessera offices are booked in the WorkSpace app. Bookings longer than 4 hours need approval from the office facilities manager.

## §2 External guests
External guests attending meetings must be registered in the WorkSpace app at least 24 hours before the visit so that security can issue visitor badges.

## §3 Room etiquette
Rooms must be left clean, with whiteboards wiped and displays turned off.

## §4 Releasing rooms
Unused bookings are released automatically if nobody checks in within 15 minutes of the start time.
