# Expense Policy FAQ

## §1 Are tips reimbursable?
Tips for meals and taxis are reimbursed up to 10% of the bill. Tips must be shown on the receipt or noted in the claim.

## §2 Can I claim laundry on a trip?
Laundry is reimbursed only on trips of five nights or longer, up to ₹1,000 per week.

## §3 What if my flight is delayed overnight?
If a flight delay forces an unplanned overnight stay and the airline does not provide a hotel, the hotel and meals are reimbursed within the normal city limits.

## §4 Can I upgrade my seat with my own miles?
Employees may upgrade with personal loyalty points or miles at no cost to the company. Loyalty points earned on business travel belong to the employee.

## §5 Is travel to the office reimbursed?
Daily commuting between home and the regular office is not reimbursed.
