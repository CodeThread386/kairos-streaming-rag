# Catering Vendor Directory — Bengaluru and Hyderabad

## §1 Bengaluru vendors
In Bengaluru, Udupi Grand Caterers provide South Indian vegetarian lunches at ₹600 per person with a minimum of 20 guests. Fusion Plate offers multi-cuisine buffets with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options at ₹950 per person with a minimum of 30 guests. Both need 72 hours' notice.

## §2 Hyderabad vendors
In Hyderabad, Deccan Delights offers Hyderabadi and continental buffets at ₹880 per person with a minimum of 25 guests and 72 hours' notice. Millet Bowl serves healthy vegetarian lunch boxes at ₹450 each with a minimum of 12 boxes.

## §3 Headcount and dietary needs
The final headcount is confirmed with the vendor 48 hours before the event. Dietary needs collected at registration must be shared with the vendor at the same time.
