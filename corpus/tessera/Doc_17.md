# AV, Equipment and Hybrid Events

## §1 Standard AV in Tessera rooms
All Tessera training rooms and boardrooms include a projector or display, a speakerphone and video conferencing. No booking is needed beyond the room itself.

## §2 Portable AV kits
Portable kits with a projector, two wireless microphones and a speaker can be borrowed from the IT Service Desk for events at partner venues. Kits must be requested at least 5 business days before the event.

## §3 Hybrid events
Events with remote participants need a dedicated AV technician to run cameras and audio. For partner venues, a technician costs ₹6,000 per day and must be booked through the IT Service Desk at least 7 business days before.

## §4 Recording
Sessions may be recorded only with the consent of all speakers. Recordings of customer workshops are stored in the Events drive for 12 months.
