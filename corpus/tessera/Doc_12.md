# Venue Cancellation and Refund Terms — Pune Partner Venues

## §1 Scope
These terms apply to bookings at partner venues in Pune. Internal Tessera rooms follow the internal room rules in section 4.

## §2 Orchid Business Hotel cancellation terms
Orchid Business Hotel allows free cancellation up to 7 days before the event. Cancelling between 7 days and 72 hours before the event costs 50% of the confirmed package value, and cancelling within 72 hours or not showing up costs 100%. The guaranteed headcount can be reduced by up to 10% without charge until 72 hours before the event.

## §3 The Deccan Courtyard cancellation terms
The Deccan Courtyard takes a 25% advance deposit at booking, which is refunded if the event is cancelled at least 21 days before. Cancelling between 21 and 7 days before forfeits the deposit, and cancelling within 7 days costs 100% of the hall hire. One free date change is allowed if requested at least 14 days before the event.

## §4 Internal rooms
There is no charge for cancelling internal Tessera rooms, but bookings must be released at least 24 hours before to avoid a no-show flag. Three no-shows in a quarter suspend room booking rights for 30 days.

## §5 Weather and force majeure
If a government advisory or a severe weather alert prevents the event, both partner venues allow the event to be rescheduled within 90 days without any penalty.
