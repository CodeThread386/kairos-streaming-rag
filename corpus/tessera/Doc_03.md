# International Travel Policy

## §1 Pre-approval
Every international trip needs pre-approval from the Vice President of the traveller's function, obtained in TravelHub at least 21 days before departure. Trips approved later than that are treated as late bookings under the Travel Booking Rules and Exceptions.

## §2 Flight class
International flights are booked in economy class when the scheduled flying time is under 8 hours and in premium economy when it is 8 hours or more. Business class requires written approval from a CXO.

## §3 Hotels and per diem abroad
International hotel stays are reimbursed up to USD 260 per night in Singapore, USD 210 per night in Germany and USD 240 per night in the United States. Daily per diem rates for meals and incidentals are listed in the Per Diem and Meal Allowances document.

## §4 Foreign currency receipt verification
All expenses paid in a foreign currency are subject to mandatory foreign currency receipt verification. Each claim must include the original itemised receipt showing the merchant, date, amount and currency. Finance converts the amount using the forex card statement rate, or the RBI reference rate on the transaction date for cash payments. Expenses without a verifiable foreign currency receipt are reimbursed only up to USD 25 per day.

## §5 Visas, insurance and security
Visa fees, mandatory health insurance and travel insurance are paid by the company. Travellers must register their itinerary with the travel security service before departure and keep the 24x7 assistance number with them.
