# Learning and Development Policy

## §1 Annual learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of ₹75,000 per financial year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry forward.

## §2 Conferences
Conference fees are paid from the learning budget. Travel to a conference follows the Travel and Expense Policy, and international conferences count as international travel that needs VP pre-approval.

## §3 Certifications
Certification exam fees are reimbursed after the employee passes the exam, up to two attempts per certification.

## §4 Learning time
Employees may spend up to 4 working hours per week on approved learning during working hours.
