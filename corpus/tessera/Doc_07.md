# Corporate Card Policy

## §1 Eligibility
Employees who travel more than four times a year, and all managers who host customer events, are eligible for a Tessera corporate card. Requests are raised in ExpenseHub and approved by the reporting manager.

## §2 Permitted use
The corporate card may be used only for business expenses that are allowed under the Travel and Expense Policy, such as hotels, meals on business trips, venue deposits and approved vendor payments. Cash withdrawals are limited to ₹10,000 per trip and are allowed only on international trips.

## §3 Monthly reconciliation
Every card transaction must be matched to a receipt and an expense line in ExpenseHub within 15 days of the statement date. Unreconciled transactions older than 45 days are deducted from salary.

## §4 Personal use
Personal purchases on the corporate card are not allowed, even if the employee intends to repay them. Accidental personal charges must be reported within 5 days and repaid through ExpenseHub.

## §5 Lost or stolen cards
Lost or stolen cards must be blocked immediately through the bank's 24x7 helpline and reported to Finance Operations within 24 hours.
