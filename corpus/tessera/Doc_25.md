# Visa and Immigration Support

## §1 Visa fees and services
Tessera pays visa fees, service charges and courier costs for business visas. Visa applications are handled by the Corporate Travel Desk's visa partner.

## §2 Processing timelines
Employees should start visa applications early: Schengen business visas take about 15 working days, Singapore business visas about 5 working days, and United States B1 visas depend on interview wait times, which can exceed 60 days.

## §3 Invitation letters
Invitation letters from the host company or customer are arranged by the traveller's manager. The Corporate Travel Desk provides a standard employment letter within 2 working days.

## §4 Passport validity
Passports must be valid for at least 6 months beyond the planned return date.
