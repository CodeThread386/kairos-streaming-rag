# IT Equipment and Device Policy

## §1 Laptop allocation and refresh
Every employee receives a company laptop on joining. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or earlier if a repair costs more than 40% of the replacement value.

## §2 Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen company device must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours so it can be remotely wiped. A police report is needed for stolen devices.

## §3 Personal devices
Personal phones may access company email and chat only through the managed work profile. Company data must not be stored on personal laptops.

## §4 Travelling with devices
On international trips, employees should carry only the devices they need and keep them in hand luggage. Customs declarations for company equipment are arranged by the IT Service Desk on request.
