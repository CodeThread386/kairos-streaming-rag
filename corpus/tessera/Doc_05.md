# Expense Claims and Receipts

## §1 Filing a claim
Expense claims are filed in ExpenseHub within 30 days of the expense, or within 30 days of the trip ending for travel. Claims filed after 60 days are rejected unless the Finance Controller grants an exception.

## §2 Receipt requirements
An itemised receipt is required for every expense above ₹500, or above USD 25 for expenses abroad. Card slips without item details are not accepted as receipts. Digital receipts and clear photographs of paper receipts are accepted.

## §3 Missing receipts
If a receipt is lost, the employee may submit a missing receipt declaration for up to ₹2,000 per claim, no more than twice per quarter. Declarations above ₹2,000 need Finance Controller approval.

## §4 Approval workflow
Claims are approved first by the reporting manager and then checked by Finance Operations. Claims above ₹1,00,000 also require Director approval.

## §5 Payment timelines
Approved claims are paid within 10 business days of final approval, by bank transfer to the salary account. Claims approved after the 20th of a month may be paid in the next payroll cycle.

## §6 Audits
Finance audits a random 10% of claims each month. Employees must keep original paper receipts for 90 days after reimbursement in case they are requested.
