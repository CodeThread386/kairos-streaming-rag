# Catering Vendor Directory — Pune

## §1 Approved vendors in Pune
External catering for events in Pune must be ordered from an approved vendor in this directory. The final headcount is confirmed with the vendor 48 hours before the event, and all listed vendors accept a variation of up to 5% on the day.

## §2 Spice Route Caterers
Spice Route Caterers serve North and South Indian buffets with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options. Lunch costs ₹780 per person and high tea costs ₹320 per person. They need a minimum of 25 guests and at least 72 hours' notice, and they can serve at the Tessera Pune office and at The Deccan Courtyard.

## §3 GreenLeaf Kitchen
GreenLeaf Kitchen offers vegetarian, vegan and Jain menus. Lunch costs ₹650 per person, with a minimum of 15 guests and 48 hours' notice. GreenLeaf can serve at the Tessera Pune office and at The Deccan Courtyard.

## §4 Brew & Bite
Brew & Bite supplies coffee, snacks and working-lunch boxes. Lunch boxes cost ₹420 each with a minimum order of 10 boxes. Same-day orders are accepted if placed before 10 AM.

## §5 Venues with in-house catering
Orchid Business Hotel in Hinjewadi does not allow outside caterers; its day delegate package includes lunch and two tea breaks. For other venues, check the venue catalog before booking a caterer.

## §6 Dietary needs and allergens
Organisers should collect dietary needs at registration. All approved vendors label common allergens, and nut-free trays can be requested when the headcount is confirmed.
