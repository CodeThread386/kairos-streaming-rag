# Remote Work and Work From Anywhere Policy

## §1 Hybrid work
Employees work from the office at least two days a week unless their role is designated fully remote.

## §2 Work from anywhere abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year, with manager approval and a tax check by the People Team. Working abroad does not count as business travel and is not reimbursed.

## §3 Home office stipend
New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of ₹25,000 and a monthly internet allowance of ₹1,500, paid with salary.

## §4 Security requirements
Remote work requires the company VPN, full-disk encryption and a screen lock of 5 minutes or less. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN connected.
