# Per Diem and Meal Allowances

## §1 How per diem works
A per diem is a fixed daily allowance for meals and incidental expenses on business trips that include an overnight stay. Receipts are not needed for the per diem itself. Meals provided by a hotel, conference or customer are deducted from the per diem.

## §2 Domestic per diem rates
The domestic per diem is ₹1,800 per day in Tier A cities, ₹1,500 per day in Tier B cities and ₹1,200 per day elsewhere. On the day of departure and the day of return, 75% of the daily rate applies.

## §3 International per diem rates
The international per diem is USD 95 per day in Singapore, USD 90 per day in Germany and the rest of the European Union, USD 100 per day in the United States, USD 105 per day in the United Kingdom and USD 70 per day in all other countries.

## §4 Day trips
Day trips without an overnight stay do not qualify for a per diem. Actual meal costs on day trips are reimbursed up to ₹750 with receipts.

## §5 Customer meals
Meals with customers are not covered by the per diem. They are claimed as business entertainment under the Gifts, Entertainment and Hospitality Policy.
