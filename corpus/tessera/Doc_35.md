# Approval Matrix

## §1 Travel approvals
Domestic trips are approved by the reporting manager. International trips need VP pre-approval at least 21 days before departure. Business class on international flights needs CXO approval.

## §2 Booking exceptions
Late bookings made less than 7 days before departure need a justification confirmed by the reporting manager, and fares more than 20% above the lowest logical fare need Director approval. Post-travel bookings need Senior Director approval.

## §3 Hotel exceptions
Hotel stays up to 20% above the city limit need Director approval, and stays more than 20% above the limit need VP approval.

## §4 Events and entertainment
Customer workshops up to ₹2,50,000 are approved by a Director, up to ₹10,00,000 by a VP, and above that by a CXO. Customer entertainment above ₹5,000 per person needs VP approval, and any hospitality for government officials needs Legal pre-approval.

## §5 Expense claims
Expense claims above ₹1,00,000 need Director approval, and missing receipt declarations above ₹2,000 need Finance Controller approval.
