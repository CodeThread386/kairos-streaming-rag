# Visitor and Guest Policy

## §1 Visitor registration
All visitors must be pre-registered by their host in the WorkSpace app and must show a government photo ID at reception.

## §2 Escorts
Visitors must be escorted by their host at all times outside the reception and meeting room areas.

## §3 Customer visitors and NDAs
Customer visitors attending confidential sessions must sign the visitor non-disclosure agreement at reception.

## §4 Wi-Fi for guests
Guests may use the guest Wi-Fi network with a daily access code from reception.
