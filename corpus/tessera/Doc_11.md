# Venue Catalog — Pune

## §1 How to use this catalog
This catalog lists approved venues for meetings, trainings and customer workshops in Pune. Capacities are given for classroom, theatre and boardroom layouts. Internal rooms are free; partner venues are paid against a purchase order.

## §2 Tessera Pune Office, Baner
The Sahyadri Training Room seats 24 in classroom layout and 32 in theatre layout. The Kaveri Boardroom seats 12. The Panshet Hall can host up to 60 people for standing receptions. Projectors and video conferencing are included, and catering must come from an approved vendor.

## §3 Orchid Business Hotel, Hinjewadi
The Lotus Hall seats 40 in classroom layout, 60 in theatre layout and 30 in U-shape. The day delegate package costs ₹2,400 per person and includes lunch, two tea breaks, a projector and Wi-Fi. The minimum booking is 20 guests, and only in-house catering is allowed.

## §4 The Deccan Courtyard, Koregaon Park
The Courtyard Hall seats 80 in theatre layout, 50 in classroom layout and 36 in boardroom layout. Studio Two seats 20. Hall hire costs ₹55,000 per day. External catering from approved vendors is allowed with a kitchen access fee of ₹5,000.

## §5 Booking procedure
Venue requests are raised with the Events Desk in the WorkSpace app at least 15 business days before the event. Provisional holds expire after 72 hours unless a purchase order is issued.

## §6 Accessibility
Orchid Business Hotel has step-free access and accessible restrooms on every floor. The Deccan Courtyard has a ramp to the Courtyard Hall, but Studio Two is on the first floor with no lift.
